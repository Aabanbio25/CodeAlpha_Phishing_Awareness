Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training Module

🔍 1. What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where attackers impersonate legitimate organizations (banks, Google, IT support) to trick users into revealing sensitive data like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal identity information.

⚠️ 2. Common Red Flags to Watch For

  • Spoofed Email Addresses: Check sender domain (e.g., support@paypa1-security.com instead of paypal.com).
  • Urgent or Threatening Language: "Account suspended in 24 hours!" or "Immediate action required!"
  • Suspicious Links & Downloads: Hover over links before clicking to verify destination URL.
  • Generic Greetings: Using "Dear Customer" instead of your actual name.

🛡️ 3. Best Practices to Prevent Phishing Attacks

  1. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all critical accounts.
  2. Never enter passwords on unverified sites or links received via unsolicited emails.
  3. Report suspicious emails to your organization's IT / Cybersecurity team immediately.
